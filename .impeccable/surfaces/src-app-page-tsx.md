# Surface Brief: Portfolio Homepage

## Scope
Single-page portfolio website — the primary and only surface.

## Visitor Mode
**Experience** — the visitor is inside the work itself. The portfolio is the artifact; the interface recedes.

## Audience & Job
- **Primary:** Recruiters/hiring managers evaluating technical candidates
- **Secondary:** Potential freelance clients
- **Job:** Quickly assess competence, understand the autodidactic journey, and decide whether to reach out

## Content & Proof
- Profile photo (GitHub avatar)
- Autodidactic narrative — the memorable differentiator
- Project examples demonstrating capability
- Contact/social links (GitHub, LinkedIn, email)
- Technology stack indicators

## Constraints
- Portuguese (Brazilian) language
- Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS 4 stack
- Static site, no CMS
- Must work across all viewports

## Direction Contract

### THESIS
Lead with the autodidactic story — a developer who exceeded his school's curriculum and chose to forge his own path. The design makes this narrative feel inevitable, not defensive.

### OWN-WORLD
Purplefolio's friendly, rounded world: `#fcfcfd` canvas, `#6e06f2` accent, Poppins headings at weight 400 with -2px tracking, 15px spacing grid, 20px+ border radius, subtle tinted shadows. The interface is approachable and professional — soft shapes, generous whitespace, single dominant purple.

### STORY
Visitor arrives, immediately understands this is a capable self-taught developer, sees projects that prove it, and has a clear path to connect. The journey from "who is this?" to "I should reach out" takes seconds.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Full-width hero on `#fcfcfd` background. Profile photo (circular, generous size) and name "Olá, eu sou Guilherme" in Poppins 400. Short tagline about the autodidactic journey. Single `#6e06f2` CTA button for contact. Technology badges as compact tags below. Clean, breathing composition — the visitor gets the story in one glance.

### FORM
Single-page scroll, sections flowing vertically. Hero → About → Projects → Contact. Each section generous with padding (60px+), clear visual hierarchy, consistent card language.

### FINISH
"unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance"
